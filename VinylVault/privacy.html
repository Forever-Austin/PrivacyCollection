Overview

VinylVault is built for collectors who care about their records. We believe your collection is yours — so we keep your data on your device and collect as little as possible.

Information We Collect

  • Collection Data: The records you catalog, including titles, artists, formats, barcodes, and notes. All data is stored locally on your device only.
  • Camera: The camera is used only to scan barcodes on record sleeves. Images are processed on-device and never uploaded.

Third-Party Services

  • Discogs: We query the public Discogs API to match barcodes and fetch record metadata. When you search or scan a record, your query is sent to Discogs. VinylVault does not have access to your Discogs account. See Discogs Privacy Policy.
  • In-App Purchases: Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) and Google (Play Store). We do not see or store your payment information.

Data Storage & Deletion

  • Your collection, wishlist, and preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
  • We do not operate servers that store your data, and we do not sell or share personal data.

Analytics & Advertising

VinylVault does not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Contact

If you have any questions, please reach out via GitHub Issues.

概述

VinylVault 专为珍视唱片的收藏家打造。我们相信你的收藏属于你自己——因此我们只把数据保存在你的设备上，并尽可能少地收集信息。

我们收集的信息

  • 收藏数据：你整理的唱片信息，包括标题、艺人、格式、条码和备注。所有数据仅保存在你的设备本地。
  • 相机：相机仅用于扫描唱片封套上的条码。图像在设备端处理，不会上传。

第三方服务

  • Discogs：我们调用公开的 Discogs API 来匹配条码并获取唱片元数据。搜索或扫描时，查询会发送给 Discogs。VinylVault 无法访问你的 Discogs 账户。详见 Discogs 隐私政策。
  • 应用内购买：购买由 Apple（App Store）和 Google（Play Store）处理。我们不会看到或存储你的支付信息。

数据存储与删除

  • 你的收藏、愿望单和偏好设置都存储在设备本地。
  • 卸载应用会删除所有本地数据。
  • 我们不运营存储你数据的服务器，也不会出售或分享个人数据。

分析与广告

VinylVault 不使用广告或第三方分析 SDK。
