VinylVault Support

Thank you for using VinylVault!

FAQ

Q: Scanning a barcode returns no results?
A: Try a different pressing or use the manual search. Some older or unofficial releases have no barcode in the Discogs database — search by artist and album title instead.

Q: How do I add a record that has no barcode?
A: Tap the search icon and look up the record by artist or album title. Old pressings often lack barcodes, and search handles those cases.

Q: Is my collection backed up?
A: Your data is stored locally on your device. We recommend using your phone's system backup to protect your data.

Q: How do I organize records into collections?
A: Open a record's details and tap the collections icon, or use the Collections tab to create and manage groups like "Jazz Corner" or "To Sell".

Q: How do I restore a purchase?
A: Go to the Profile tab and tap "Restore Purchases".
