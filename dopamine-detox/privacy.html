Dopamine Detox Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

Data Collection

Dopamine Detox does not collect, store, or upload any personal information.

All data — including your detox progress, goal settings, relapse history, and blocked app selections — is stored exclusively on your device. We use Apple's SharedPreferences and App Group mechanisms solely to synchronize data between the main app and its widget extension. We have no access to this data, and no servers or third-party services ever receive it.

Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

In-App Purchases

The Pro upgrade is handled entirely through Apple's App Store In-App Purchase system. The transaction is processed by Apple; we only receive an anonymous purchase validation receipt to unlock Pro features. We do not receive your payment information.

Screen Time Permissions

This app uses Apple's Screen Time API (FamilyControls / ManagedSettings) to implement app blocking. This process is controlled by iOS; the app only reads the list of apps you authorize in order to set blocking rules. We do not access your actual screen time usage data.

Children's Privacy

This app is not designed for children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
