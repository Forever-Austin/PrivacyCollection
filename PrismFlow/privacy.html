Prism Flow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-09

Prism Flow is a minimal habit tracker and data-visualization tool. We make a firm commitment: your data belongs to you alone. We never collect, store, or share any personal information.

What We Collect

We collect nothing. Prism Flow has no account system, no servers, no analytics, and no advertising SDK. Everything runs entirely on your device.

Data Storage

All your habits, check-in records, notes, and other data are stored only in your device's local storage (SQLite). Preferences (theme, nickname, etc.) are stored locally on your device. We do not use cloud sync and never upload any data.

Local Notifications

When you create a habit reminder, the app schedules local notifications on your device. Notification content is generated locally and is never sent to us or any third party.

In-App Purchase

If you upgrade to Pro, payment is processed by Apple (App Store). We only receive a success/failure status, never your payment details. Subscriptions can be restored after reinstall via "Restore Purchase" in the app.

Your Rights

Because we collect no data, there is nothing of yours for us to delete or share. You can export your own check-in records (CSV) from within the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all local data on your device.

Children's Privacy

This app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information, so no special handling is required.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: privacy@prismflow.app

Prism Flow — 隐私政策

更新日期：2026-08-09

Prism Flow（棱镜·流光） 是一款极简习惯追踪与数据可视化工具。我们郑重承诺：你的数据只属于你，我们绝不收集、存储或分享任何个人信息。

我们收集什么

我们不收集任何数据。 Prism Flow 没有账号系统、没有服务器、没有分析工具、没有广告 SDK。所有功能完全在你的设备本地运行。

数据存储

你的习惯、打卡记录、备注等全部数据，仅保存在你设备的本地存储中（SQLite）。偏好设置（主题、昵称等）仅保存在设备本地。我们不使用云同步，也不会上传任何数据。

本地通知

你创建习惯提醒时，App 会在你的设备上安排本地通知。通知内容仅在设备本地生成，不会发送给我们或任何第三方。

内购

若你选择升级 Pro，付款由 Apple（App Store）处理。我们仅收到购买是否成功的状态，不接触你的支付信息。订阅可通过 App 内"恢复购买"在重装后找回。

你的权利

由于我们不收集数据，我们没有任何你的数据可以删除或导出给第三方。你随时可以在 App 内导出自己的打卡记录（CSV）。卸载 App 会删除设备上的全部本地数据。

儿童隐私

本 App 适用于所有年龄用户，不收集任何个人信息。

联系我们

如有隐私相关问题，请联系：privacy@prismflow.app
