Thank you for using Nudge!

FAQ

Q: Notifications not appearing?
A: Please ensure notification permissions are granted in Settings → Notification Permissions. Also check iOS system settings for Nudge notifications.

Q: How to record a voice nudge?
A: Go to Settings → Voice Recording and grant microphone permission to start recording.

Q: Is data backed up?
A: Data is currently stored locally only. We recommend using iOS system backup to protect your data.
