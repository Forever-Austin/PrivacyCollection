Nudge Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

Information We Collect

Nudge does not use account systems and does not collect personally identifiable information. Here is the data we handle:

  • Reminder Data: Titles, descriptions, times, repeat modes, and completion records you create. All data is stored locally on your device only.
  • Voice Nudges: Voice recordings you create are stored exclusively on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

Third-Party Services

Nudge uses the following third-party services, which may collect anonymous data for advertising and analytics:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device information for ad targeting. See Google Privacy Policy for details.

Data Storage & Transmission

All reminder data is stored on your device using SharedPreferences and the local file system. Voice recordings are stored in the app sandbox. We do not upload your data to any remote servers.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally, you can at any time:

  • Delete any reminder or recording within the app
  • Uninstall the app to remove all data

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy. All changes will be published on this page.
